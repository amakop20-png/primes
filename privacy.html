Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 18, 2025

Your privacy is of utmost importance to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how Nura SQ collects, safeguards, and utilizes your personal information when you access our virtual number platform, SMS delivery APIs, and related services.

1 Information We Collect

We only collect data necessary to provide reliable telecommunication services:

  • Account Information: Name, username, email address, phone number, and hashed authentication credentials.
  • Transaction & Order Data: Order IDs, requested services, country origins, wallet funding receipts, and activation timestamps.
  • Technical Diagnostics: IP addresses, browser user-agents, device categories, and error logs collected solely for system integrity and anti-fraud monitoring.

2 How We Use Your Information

  • To allocate virtual telephone numbers and route SMS verifications accurately.
  • To maintain your wallet balance, process top-ups, and generate transaction histories.
  • To detect and prevent fraudulent account creation, automated bot abuse, or illicit traffic.
  • To communicate crucial service updates, maintenance windows, and respond to support requests.

3 Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to advertisers. Information is disclosed strictly under the following circumstances:

  • Telecom & Carrier Networks: Upstream carrier partners required to provision virtual numbers and deliver SMS messages.
  • Payment Gateways: Secure PCI-compliant processors who handle billing transactions without storing raw card credentials on our servers.
  • Legal Requirements: When compelled by valid legal subpoenas, court orders, or applicable regulations.

4 Data Security & Retention

We implement enterprise-grade security protocols, including TLS encryption in transit, strict database access controls, and salted password hashing. Verification SMS logs are retained only as long as necessary to confirm order fulfillment, after which they are systematically purged.

5 Cookies & Local Storage

Our platform uses essential local storage and session tokens strictly to keep you authenticated, remember your UI theme preferences (light/dark mode), and maintain your active session state.

6 Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to access the personal data held on your account, request corrections to inaccurate details, or request full account closure and deletion of your profile by reaching out to our privacy compliance officer.

Data Privacy Questions?

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data protection rights, please contact our data team.
