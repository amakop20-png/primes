Support Overview

Welcome to the NuraXQ Help Center. We are here to support you with instant SMS verification delivery, service availability, virtual numbers, and account troubleshooting.

Common Issues & Questions

  • Why didn’t I receive my SMS code? Verify that the third-party service has sent the code. SMS delivery typically takes between 5 to 60 seconds depending on provider traffic.
  • Can I use my number for multiple services? Standard numbers are dedicated to a single requested service. If you need multi-service numbers, select rental options.
  • How long does a number stay active? Activation windows range from 15 to 20 minutes for single verification purchases.
  • What should I do if my purchase failed? Failed activations are automatically cancelled and your wallet balance remains untouched or is instantly refunded.

Troubleshooting Steps

Start by confirming that the sending service is supported in your target country. If you encounter repeated delays, cancel the activation from your dashboard to release the credit and attempt with an alternative carrier network.
