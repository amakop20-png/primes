Refund Policy –

Daveslogo

Last Updated: 18-07-2025

At JoshSMS, customer satisfaction is a priority. However, due to the digital and instant nature of our services, all successful purchases are final.

✅ When Refunds Are Allowed

  • No SMS received
  • Major technical issues reported within 72 hours
  • Issue not resolved within 72 hours

❌ Non-Refundable Cases

  • Service already used or credited
  • Platform or user-side issues
  • Request made after 7 days

📨 How to Request

Email: support@joshsms.com

Include your order ID, affected number/service, and a brief issue description. Screenshots help.

⏳ Refund Processing

Refunds may be returned to the original payment method or as JoshSMS credit. Processing times depend on your provider.

Questions? Reach us at support@joshsms.com
