Refund Policy

Last Updated: July 18, 2025

At Nura SQ, customer satisfaction and dependable SMS verification are our foremost priorities. Because virtual numbers are instant digital goods, we have established clear guidelines below for when refunds and balance credits are applicable.

When Refunds Are Eligible

You are eligible for an immediate automatic balance refund or support credit in the following circumstances:

  • No SMS Received: If no verification code arrives before the activation window expires (or if you cancel before an SMS is received), your balance is immediately restored.
  • Service Carrier Outage: If a technical outage on upstream carrier routes prevents message delivery and is verified by our system diagnostics.
  • Duplicate Payment Deductions: If an automated gateway billing glitch leads to double-deduction on a single top-up order.

Non-Refundable Scenarios

Refunds or credit reversals cannot be issued in the following cases:

  • Successfully Delivered SMS: Once an SMS verification code has been successfully received by the number, the order is deemed fulfilled and non-refundable.
  • Third-Party Account Restrictions: If the destination service (e.g. WhatsApp, Telegram, Google) denies your registration due to prior device bans or application-level restrictions outside our control.
  • Expired Unreported Requests: Refund claims submitted more than 7 days after the affected transaction timestamp.

How to Request a Manual Refund

If your issue is not resolved by our automatic cancellation engine, please contact support with the following information:

  • Your registered account email or username.
  • The Order ID or Transaction Reference.
  • The virtual phone number and target service requested.
  • A concise description or screenshot of the error encountered.

Refund Processing & Payouts

Approved refunds are typically returned as instant Nura SQ account balance credit, allowing immediate reuse for alternative numbers. For direct fiat payment reversals (via the original payment processor), processing takes between 3 to 7 business days depending on your banking provider.

Need help with an order refund?

Our billing support specialists are available around the clock to review your transactions and ensure fair resolution.
